Privacy

Interview Sim · last updated 24 October 2026

Interview Sim is a private, invite-only practice tool run by one person for a small group of invited users. It is not a commercial service and shows no ads.

What is stored

  • Your Google account email and name, used only to sign you in and match you to your invite.
  • Your practice interviews: chosen topics, the questions, the text of your answers, scores and feedback.
  • Custom topics you create, and daily usage counts used to enforce fair-use limits.
  • Basic sign-in records (time, IP address, browser) so the owner can see who has used the app.

Voice recordings are not stored. Voice and display preferences are saved in your own browser only.

Who processes it

  • Groq generates questions and feedback from your answer text, and transcribes your voice when the Whisper option is used.
  • Your browser's built-in speech recognition (when used) may send audio to your browser's vendor, such as Google for Chrome.
  • Google handles sign-in. Cloudflare carries the connection to the server.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Data is kept on the owner's own server.

Your choices

You can delete any interview from your History at any time. To have your account and all of your data removed, email ard121001@gmail.com.
